Rules

50KB decoration budget (UI/UX #50). All decorative assets on a page — illustrations, patterns, mascot — must stay under 50KB total. Prefer inline SVG + CSS gradients over images.
Touch targets ≥ 44px. Every button, chip and tab meets the 44px minimum (.tbtn-sm{min-height:44px}).
Dark mode is a first-class theme. Every new component must define [data-theme="dark"] styles; theme choice persists in localStorage.
No hard-coded user-facing text. All copy goes through data-i18n keys → centralized dictionaries → 10 languages.
Motion respects users. Decorative animation pauses under prefers-reduced-motion; transitions stay under 300ms.

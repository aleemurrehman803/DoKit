Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

DoKit collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, and no advertising cookies.

Your files stay yours

Image tools process files entirely on your device, inside your browser. Files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

What is stored locally

Theme, language, and notification sign-ups in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them completely.

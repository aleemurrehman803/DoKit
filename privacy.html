Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

The short version

DoKit collects as little as possible. Your files are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device. We don't run cross-site trackers, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads. If you create an account or email us, we keep just what's needed to run the service — and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

🔧 How "100% in your browser" actually works

When you drop an image into a DoKit tool, here's what happens — in plain words:

  1. Your file stays on your device. It is read by the web page itself, using your browser's built-in capabilities — the same way a calculator app adds numbers without sending them anywhere.
  2. The work happens locally. Resizing, compressing or counting is done by code running on your phone or computer, not on our servers.
  3. Nothing is uploaded. We don't have a "receive files" server for tools at all — there is simply nowhere for your file to go.
  4. The result is yours. The download comes straight from your browser's memory to your device.

The only things that ever leave your device are the things you explicitly choose: creating an account, sending a contact message, or (optionally) anonymous usage counts that help us know which tools people love. That's it.

What we collect

  • Account info — only if you sign up: your name, email address, and (stored securely, never in plain text) your password.
  • Contact messages — what you send us through the contact form or by email, so we can reply.
  • Notify-list emails — if you ask to be notified about new tools, we keep your name and email solely for that purpose.
  • Usage analytics — privacy-friendly, aggregated statistics (for example, which tools are popular). No cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles.

What we NEVER collect

  • Your files. Image tools process everything locally in your browser. Files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Passwords in plain text. Passwords are stored using one-way hashing only.
  • Data for advertisers. We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

How we use it

  • To provide and maintain the service (accounts, typing progress, preferences).
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To send launch notifications — only if you opted in, and you can unsubscribe from any email with one click.

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted services to run DoKit. They process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database. Google Privacy Policy
  • Payment providers — for Pro/Team subscriptions, payments are processed securely by our payment partners. Stripe Privacy Policy (card payments)

Cookies & local storage

DoKit stores small pieces of data in your browser to remember things like your theme, language, and preferences. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, just email malikjalil014@gmail.com — we'll respond within a few days.

Children's privacy

DoKit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

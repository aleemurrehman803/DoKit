Pro tips

  • Use keyword density for SEO The top-10 keywords table shows how often each word appears. Keep your main keyword around 1–2% of the total — higher than that looks like stuffing to search engines.
  • Plan by speaking time At about 150 words per minute, 300 words is roughly 2 minutes of speech. Use the live speaking-time stat to size scripts, presentations, and videos.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

Never. All counting happens in your browser with JavaScript — your text never leaves your device.

How is reading time calculated?

Based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute; speaking time is calculated at 130 words per minute, the average speech rate.

Does it work with Urdu text?

Yes. Word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts work for Urdu, and common Urdu words are filtered out of the keyword list.

What counts as a word?

Any sequence of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. Hyphenated words count as one word; numbers like “2026” count as words too.

    How to make a board exam photo

    1. Find your board

      Type your board or university name and pick it from the list. Check the spec card for the exact size, background and KB.

    2. Upload your photo

      Drop a clear, front-facing photo into the box. A plain background matching your board's requirement works best.

    3. Auto-fit to board specs

      The photo is auto-cropped to your board's exact dimensions and compressed to fit the KB target — your face stays fully in frame.

    4. Download

      Download the JPG and upload it straight to your board's admission or registration portal.

    Pro tips

    • Use a plain background photo Boards reject busy backgrounds. Take the photo against a plain wall in the colour your board asks for (usually white or blue).
    • Face the camera, neutral expression Look straight at the camera with eyes open and a neutral expression — the same rules as a passport photo.
    • Check “typical” specs with your board Boards marked “typical” haven't published official specs — the common Pakistani exam standard is used. Confirm the size with your board before submitting.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which boards and universities are included?

    BISE Peshawar, BISE Swat, BISE Mardan, BISE Kohat and BISE D.I. Khan (300×300 px, white background), BIE Karachi (passport size, blue background), BBISE Quetta (white background), BISE Bahawalpur (150×200 px, 8–22 KB, blue background), FBISE, LUMS, NUST, UET Lahore, Punjab University, University of Karachi, IBA Karachi, COMSATS, AIOU, AJK Board and more. Boards marked “typical” use the common Pakistani exam standard — please confirm with your board.

    What does the verified badge mean?

    “Verified” means the size, background and file-size limit were read from an official board notification or the board's own website. “Typical” means the board hasn't published specs, so the common Pakistani exam standard is used — confirm with your board before submitting.

    What if my board is missing?

    Use the generic “Pakistani admission (typical)” preset, or type your board's width, height and KB target into the custom fields — every field is editable.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    Never. Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript — your photo never leaves your device.

    How is the photo cropped?

    The tool auto-crops to your board's exact dimensions, keeping the head fully in frame with headroom — your face is never altered or cut.

    What file format do I get?

    A JPG sized to your board's exact pixels and compressed to fit at or under the KB target, ready to upload to the board's portal.

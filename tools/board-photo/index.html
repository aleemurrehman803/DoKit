Board Photo

Choose your board or university, upload a photo, and get it in the exact size and file size required. Everything happens in your browser — your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Download photo

What this tool does not do: it crops and resizes your photo to the board's size and fits it into the file-size limit on a solid background. It does not remove backgrounds and cannot check photo rules beyond the published size and file-size limits. Board rules change — always check your board's latest notice before submitting.

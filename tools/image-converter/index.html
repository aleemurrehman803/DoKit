    How to convert images

    1. Add up to 10 images

      Drag & drop your PNG, JPEG, or WebP files into the box, or click to browse. Each file can be up to 25 MB.

    2. Pick the output format

      Choose PNG, JPEG, or WebP from the dropdown. JPEG and WebP get a quality slider; PNG is always lossless.

    3. Download each file

      Every converted file gets its own download button. A failed file never stops the rest of the queue.

    Pro tips

    • Convert PNG screenshots to WebP Screenshots and graphics with text stay crisp as WebP but shrink dramatically — often 50–70% smaller than PNG, with zero visible loss.
    • Keep PNG for editing, JPEG for sharing PNG is lossless, so it is the best archive format when you plan to edit later. Convert to JPEG or WebP only for the final copy you share or upload.

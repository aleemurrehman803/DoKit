Frequently asked questions

Are the passwords sent anywhere?

Never. Passwords are generated with your browser's crypto-grade random generator — nothing leaves your device.

How long should my password be?

At least 16 characters for important accounts. Longer passwords with mixed character types are exponentially harder to crack.

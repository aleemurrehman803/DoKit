How to compress an image

  1. Upload your image

    Drag & drop a PNG, JPEG, or WebP file (up to 25 MB) into the box, or click to browse.

  2. Choose quality or a target size

    Drag the quality slider and watch the live size estimate, or switch to target-size mode and type the maximum KB you want.

  3. Compare and download

    Drag the comparison slider to check quality, then download your smaller image — watermark-free.

Pro tips

  • The 80–85 sweet spot For JPEG photos, quality 80–85 usually halves the file size compared to 95+, with differences only visible when zoomed in. Drag the comparison slider to check yourself.
  • Try WebP for bigger savings WebP keeps the same visual quality as JPEG at roughly 25–35% smaller size — ideal for web images. Choose JPEG only when compatibility with old software matters.

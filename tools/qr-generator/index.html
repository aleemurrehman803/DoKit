Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

Never. The QR code is drawn in your browser with JavaScript — your text never leaves your device.

What can I put in a QR code?

Any text up to a few thousand characters: website URLs, plain messages, phone numbers, or email addresses.

Pro tips

  • Resize before you upload Websites rarely display images wider than 1600 px, so downsizing first makes pages load faster and uploads finish sooner — with no visible difference.
  • JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics JPEG at quality 85–90 keeps photos small and crisp; PNG stays sharp for logos and screenshots with text. WebP usually beats both on file size.

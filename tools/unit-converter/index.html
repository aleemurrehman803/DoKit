Frequently asked questions

Does the converter need the internet?

No. All conversions are pure JavaScript math in your browser — it even works offline once the page is cached.

Which units are supported?

Length (mm, cm, m, km, inch, foot, mile), weight (g, kg, oz, lb), temperature (°C, °F, K), and data (KB, MB, GB, TB).

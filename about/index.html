About DoKit

DoKit is a toolbox that lives in your browser — honest, free, and private. No ads, no tracking, no accounts standing between you and your work.

Our story

DoKit started with a simple frustration: everyday online tools were stuffed with pop-up ads, demanded an account before doing anything useful, or quietly uploaded your files to a server you never asked about. So we built the opposite. Every DoKit tool runs entirely on your device, inside your browser. Your photos are resized on your phone. Your words are counted on your laptop. There is no server to breach, because your files never leave your hands.

Our mission

Give students, freelancers, and teachers — in Pakistan and around the world — professional-quality tools that respect them. Free forever for the basics, private by design, and available in 10 languages so more people can use them comfortably.

The founder

Malik Jalil Ur Rehman Khokhar designed and built every tool and every page on DoKit — from the image utilities to TypeMaster, the design to the code. One maker, full responsibility: when you write to us, your message reaches the person who built the thing you're asking about.

What we believe

🔒 Privacy first

Your files are processed on your device and never sent anywhere. No tracking scripts, no advertising cookies, nothing to opt out of — because there's nothing to opt into.

🚀 Free forever

Every basic tool is free with no usage caps and no watermarks. Optional Pro plans fund development — they never take away what was free.

How Much Does AI Consulting Cost?

October 2026 · AI opinions

Ask five firms what AI consulting costs and you'll get five wildly different answers. That isn't necessarily dishonesty — it reflects genuinely different scopes. Understanding the models is the fastest way to compare quotes fairly.

The three common models

  • Hourly advisory. You pay for an expert's time, usually for advice, reviews, and short guidance. Good for a second opinion; bad for open-ended projects, because the meter never stops.
  • Fixed-scope projects. One defined job — for example, one automated workflow — for one agreed price. This is the easiest model to compare, because you can demand the same deliverables from every bidder.
  • Monthly retainers. Ongoing help: monitoring, improvements, and support. Sensible only after a first project has already proven value.

What actually drives the price

Four things move a quote more than anything else: how messy your data is, how many other systems the automation must connect to, how much human review you require, and how much support you expect after launch. A quote that ignores all four is guessing.

How to compare quotes honestly

  • Demand the same written scope from everyone: deliverables, timeline, and what "done" means.
  • Ask what happens when it breaks — who fixes it, and at what cost.
  • Ask who owns the setup. If you can't run it without the consultant, you are renting, not buying.
  • Be suspicious of any quote that can't explain its own price in one paragraph.

Why ads are a bad way to buy this

Consulting keywords are among the most expensive clicks in advertising, which is why serious comparison happens through guides, referrals, and documented results — not sponsored posts. If someone's main credential is an ad, keep looking.

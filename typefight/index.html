Frequently asked questions

What is TypeFight?

TypeFight turns typing into a sport: players enter a battle room, everyone types the same text live, and the fastest, most accurate typist takes the pot. Pure skill — no luck, no pay-to-win.

When does TypeFight launch?

It is planned for Phase 5, after the typing school and core tools are complete. Building it needs a real backend — matchmaking, payments, and fraud review cannot run in a browser alone. Leave your email above and we will tell you when battles open.

Will it cost money?

Entering a battle will cost a small entry fee — that fee is exactly what forms the prize pot. Watching and practicing will always be free.

How will winners be paid?

Winnings will be paid out through standard digital payment methods to the winner's verified account, and every pot and payout will be publicly auditable.

How is cheating prevented?

Battles use live keystroke analysis that rejects paste and automation patterns, one verified account per person, screen-plus-camera verification for high-stake finals, and transparent, auditable pots.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DoKit.

Is DoKit free?

Yes! All basic tools are completely free. Pro plans unlock premium features.

Where do my files go?

Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded to our servers. Your privacy is guaranteed.

Do I need an account?

No for basic tools. Account needed for saving progress, coins, and premium features.

Which browsers are supported?

All modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.

Is there a mobile app?

No native app yet, but the website works perfectly on mobile.

What is TypeMaster?

Our typing tutor section with lessons, tests, and games.

How do I earn coins?

Compete in TypeFight typing battles, check in every day for streak rewards, and invite friends with your referral link. Coins unlock premium perks and extras — no purchase ever needed.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer, USDT, and credit/debit cards for Pro and Team plans — so you can pay the way that's easiest for you.

Is my data safe?

Yes. DoKit is privacy-first: your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded, and we never sell your data. We show no ads and run no tracking scripts. This preview build stores nothing on a server.

How do I contact support?

Email us anytime at malikjalil014@gmail.com — we read every message personally and usually reply within 24–48 hours.

Still have a question? Contact us — we'd love to hear from you.

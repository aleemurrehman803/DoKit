DoKit is a toolbox that lives in your browser — honest, free, and private. No ads, no tracking, no accounts standing between you and your work.

Our story

DoKit started with a simple frustration: everyday online tools were stuffed with pop-up ads, demanded an account before doing anything useful, or quietly uploaded your files to a server you never asked about.

So we built the opposite. Every DoKit tool runs entirely on your device, inside your browser. Your photos are resized on your phone. Your words are counted on your laptop. There is no server to breach, because your files never leave your hands.

Our mission is simple: give students, freelancers, and teachers — in Pakistan and around the world — professional-quality tools that respect them. Free forever for the basics, private by design, and available in 10 languages so more people can use them comfortably.

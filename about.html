About DoKit

Simple tools, honestly built.

DoKit started with a simple frustration: everyday online tools were either stuffed with ads, demanded an account before doing anything useful, or quietly uploaded your files to a server you never asked about.

So we built the opposite — a toolbox that lives entirely in your browser. Your photos are resized on your device. Your words are counted on your device. There is no server to breach, because there is no server.

🔒

No uploads, ever

Files are processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

🚪

No accounts, no gates

Open a tool and use it. That's the whole onboarding.

🌐

Two languages

Full English and اردو interfaces, including right-to-left layout.

About DoKit

Simple tools, honestly built — by one person, for everyone.

Our story

DoKit is a free, browser-based tool hub built by Malik Jalil Ur Rehman Khokhar. It began with a simple frustration: everyday online tools were stuffed with ads, demanded an account before doing anything useful, or quietly uploaded your files to a server you never asked about.

DoKit is built for the people who need honest tools the most — students preparing documents against a deadline, freelancers resizing client photos at midnight, teachers making worksheets for their classes — in Pakistan and everywhere else. If it runs in your browser and costs nothing, it belongs here.

Everything happens on your device. Your photos are resized locally, your words are counted locally. There is no server to breach, no account to forget, no tracking to escape — because there is no server.

Who makes DoKit?

DoKit is designed and built by Malik Jalil Ur Rehman Khokhar — every tool, every page. One maker, full responsibility: if something breaks or confuses you, it is on us to fix it.

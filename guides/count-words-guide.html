Guide

How to count words

Instant word counts, reading time and keyword stats for essays, articles, captions and scripts.

Open Word Counter

Steps

  1. 1. Paste or type your text

    Drop an essay, article, caption or script into the box. Stats update live as you type.

  2. 2. Read the headline stats

    Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time appear instantly.

  3. 3. Check keyword density

    See your top 10 keywords — handy for SEO checks and spotting overused words.

  4. 4. Hit your target length

    Writing to a limit? Watch the counter as you trim or expand to land exactly on target.

  5. 5. Copy the report

    One click copies the full statistics to your clipboard for reports and submissions.

Pro tips

  • Reading time ≈ words ÷ 200. Speaking time is slower — about 130 words per minute.
  • Watch keyword stuffing. If one word dominates the top-10 list, vary your vocabulary.
  • Works with Urdu text too. Paste Urdu script and get accurate counts.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No — counting happens entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

What counts as a word?

Sequences of letters/numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. Hyphenated words count as one.

Can I count words in a PDF or Word file?

Copy the text out of the document and paste it into the counter — formatting is ignored, only text is counted.

    کسی حد کے مطابق لکھ رہے ہیں؟ ہدف پر عین اترنے کے لیے تراشتے یا بڑھاتے وقت کاؤنٹر دیکھیں۔

ماہرانہ مشورے

  • پڑھنے کا وقت ≈ الفاظ ÷ 200۔ بولنے کا وقت سست ہوتا ہے — تقریباً 130 الفاظ فی منٹ۔
  • کلیدی لفظ بھرنے سے ہوشیار رہیں۔ اگر ایک لفظ ٹاپ 10 فہرست پر حاوی ہو تو ذخیرہ الفاظ متنوع کریں۔
  • اردو متن کے ساتھ بھی کام کرتا ہے۔ اردو رسم الخط چسپاں کریں اور درست گنتی حاصل کریں۔

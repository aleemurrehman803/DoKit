How to convert text case

Fix ALL-CAPS headings, lowercase SHOUTING, and format titles perfectly — six case styles, one click each.

Open Case Converter

Steps

  1. 1. Paste your text

    Drop any text into the box — headings, paragraphs, lists. Everything stays in your browser.

  2. 2. Pick a case style

    Choose from UPPER CASE, lower case, Title Case, Sentence case, aLtErNaTiNg or iNVERSE.

  3. 3. Preview the result

    The converted text appears instantly. Toggle between styles to compare.

  4. 4. Copy or download

    One click copies the result to your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

Pro tips

  • Title Case is for headings — it capitalizes every major word, perfect for article and video titles.
  • Sentence case fixes SHOUTING — paste ALL-CAPS text and get clean, readable sentences.
  • Works with Urdu text too — Urdu script has no case, so it passes through unchanged while English words convert.

Frequently asked questions

What is iNVERSE cAsE?

It flips every letter's case — uppercase becomes lowercase and vice versa. Mostly for fun or stylized text.

How does Title Case handle hyphenated words?

Each part of a hyphenated word is capitalized (e.g. “well-known” → “Well-Known”).

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No — conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Does it work with Urdu text?

Yes. Urdu script has no uppercase/lowercase, so it stays as-is while any English words in the mix are converted.

How to count words

Instant word counts, reading time and keyword stats for essays, articles, captions and scripts.

Open Word Counter →

Open Word Counter

Steps

  1. 1. Paste or type your text Drop an essay, article, caption or script into the box. Stats update live as you type.
  2. 2. Read the headline stats Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time appear instantly.
  3. 3. Check keyword density See your top 10 keywords — handy for SEO checks and spotting overused words.
  4. 4. Hit your target length Writing to a limit? Watch the counter as you trim or expand to land exactly on target.
  5. 5. Copy the report One click copies the full statistics to your clipboard for reports and submissions.

Pro tips

  • Reading time ≈ words ÷ 200. Speaking time is slower — about 130 words per minute.
  • Watch keyword stuffing. If one word dominates the top-10 list, vary your vocabulary.
  • Works with Urdu text too. Paste Urdu script and get accurate counts.

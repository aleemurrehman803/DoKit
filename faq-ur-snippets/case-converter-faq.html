Frequently asked questions

How does Title Case handle hyphenated words?

Each part gets capitalized separately — “well-known” becomes “Well-Known”. This matches standard title-case rules.

What is iNVERSE cAsE?

It flips the case of every letter: uppercase becomes lowercase and lowercase becomes uppercase. Numbers and symbols stay the same.

Does it work with Urdu text?

Urdu script has no upper/lowercase distinction, so Urdu words pass through unchanged while Latin words are converted.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

Never — conversion happens entirely in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my image get?

It depends on the photo, but quality 80 JPEG is usually 40–70% smaller than the original with no visible difference. PNG screenshots often shrink even more when converted to WebP.

What does target-size mode do?

You type a maximum file size (for example 200 KB) and DoKit automatically searches for the highest quality setting that fits under it — handy for upload limits on forms and profiles.

Will compression remove metadata?

Yes. The image is redrawn on a fresh canvas, which strips EXIF data including GPS location.

Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?

No daily limits and no sign-up. Compress one image at a time here, or use the Image Converter to batch-convert up to 10 images at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the lessons work?

Each lesson teaches 2–3 new keys through guided drills. Finish a lesson with 90%+ accuracy to unlock the next one, moving from beginner home-row basics to full advanced passages.

Is TypeMaster free?

Yes — all beginner lessons and 3 timed tests per day are free forever, no credit card required. Pro and School plans unlock unlimited tests, analytics, and certificates.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn and take tests without signing up. A free account only adds saved history, XP, badges, and streaks across your devices.

How is WPM calculated?

We use gross WPM: all typed characters (including errors) divided by 5 — one 'word' — then divided by minutes. Net WPM subtracts uncorrected errors. Both are shown on every result screen.

Can I practice on my phone?

Yes — the whole site works in your phone's browser, so lessons and tests run anywhere. For the fastest progress though, a real keyboard is recommended.

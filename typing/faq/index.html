Typing FAQ

How typing on DoKit works, in plain language.

How do the lessons work?

There are 44 lessons in four groups: Beginner (keys, one group at a time), Intermediate (words, sentences and fluency), Advanced (paragraphs, numbers and symbols) and Stories (longer reading). Each lesson shows the keys it teaches and gives practice lines.

Do I need an account to practise?

No. Tests, lessons, practice and games all work without an account, and your results save on this device only. Accounts with cloud sync arrive with the backend in Phase 3.

How is WPM calculated?

WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) per minute. Accuracy = correct characters ÷ characters typed. Speed follows accuracy: type correctly first, speed comes with practice.

What is the difference between the test and practice?

The test is timed (30, 60 or 120 seconds) and gives a final score. Practice is untimed: you finish when the passage is complete, and you can also paste your own text.

Can I learn Urdu typing here?

Yes. The Urdu page has a standard phonetic layout: Roman letters convert to Urdu, plus an on-screen Urdu keyboard and Urdu practice passages.

Do certificates exist?

Certificates are issued for completed typing courses. Issuing and verification connect with the backend in Phase 3 — this build never shows a fake certificate.

Is my typing data uploaded anywhere?

No. Results and lesson completions are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Clearing your browser data clears them too.
